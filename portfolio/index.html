About

I'm Yeasin — I design brand marks and interfaces, then build them.

It started with a sketchbook and a stubborn question: why do some logos feel inevitable while others just sit there? Chasing that took me into identity work — a wordmark for a coffee roaster, a redesign for a medical centre, marks for a law group and a supply company. Small teams, mostly, who needed an identity that could do some work for them.

Then I got curious about what happens after the handoff, so I learned to build. Computer technology at IET, a government-certified Level 3 in web design and development, and now a BSc in CSE at BUBT. These days I'm about as comfortable in a code editor as in Illustrator — which mostly means I design things I can actually ship.

What I keep coming back to is restraint. Clear type, honest spacing, and nothing on the page that isn't doing a job. If I can delete it and nothing breaks, it goes.
